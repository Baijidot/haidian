Public Interface Rights

Readable

Chinese, English, pictogram, audio/easy-read and accessible information explain each suggestion's limits.

Questionable

A provenance passport shows version, use, limits, reviewer, correction channel and removal condition.

Reversible

Refuse identification, withdraw consent or switch to a person; severe incidents, unauthorized collection or failed human channels stop the pilot.
